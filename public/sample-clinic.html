Gentle dental care for the whole family

Smile Studio has looked after families in Erandwane and Kothrud since 2011. We keep appointments on time, explain every treatment before we start and never push work you do not need.

What we do

Routine check-ups and cleaning, tooth-coloured fillings, root canal treatment, crowns and bridges, clear aligners, teeth whitening and dental implants. Children are welcome from their first tooth, and we keep a quiet room for patients who feel anxious about the dentist.

Most check-ups take thirty minutes. If you need treatment, we give you a written plan with the cost of each step so there are no surprises when you come back.

Meet the team

Dr. Asha Kulkarni (BDS, MDS Prosthodontics) leads the practice and handles implants and crowns. Dr. Rohan Joshi looks after root canals and emergency visits, and our hygienist Meera runs the cleaning and gum care clinic every weekday morning.
